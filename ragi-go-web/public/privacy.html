Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

Welcome to Raagi GO. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Delivery Information: Physical address and contact details for order fulfillment.
  • Order History: Details of the products you purchase through our app.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Process and deliver your breakfast orders.
  • Send order status updates via SMS or Notifications.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of the Raagi GO app.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share information with:

  • Delivery Personnel: Only the necessary details (Name, Address, Phone) required to deliver your order.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your connection to our services is encrypted (HTTPS), and personal data is stored securely using Firebase.

Users have the right to request the deletion of their personal data. You can contact us at the details below to request account and data removal.
